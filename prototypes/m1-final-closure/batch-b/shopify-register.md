## Resolution

All21 requested categories are covered explicitly;30 rows separate architecture decisions, implementation, measurement and submission. Current account/Shop support, generic Custom Liquid, eligible foundation, font/color constraints, metaobject defaults and package exclusions are recorded for future work rather than assumed present. Product/architecture proof can pass traceability without production Liquid. Live `@app` remains an integration gate because the controlling M1 contract calls for host insertion/resilience proof, not live platform certification; the narrower Evidence Rail engineering verdict is retained. Official requirements remain subject to change. This register does not waive a requirement or authorize M2.
