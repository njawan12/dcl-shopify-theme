# Originality strategy

## Thesis: operability plus a launch narrative

The product is not a Dawn/Horizon reskin or a collage of Prestige, Impulse, Symmetry, Broadcast, Impact, or other competitor sections. Its hypothesis is that **merchant operability** can be recognizable product design: job-led starting compositions, semantic controls, strong defaults, bounded choices, mobile-specific content priority, optional structured content, and a coordinated decision grammar: **Reveal → Explain → Prove → Compare → Act**.

The grammar is internal design and architecture language, not required merchant-facing copy and never a compulsory five-section funnel. A page may omit, combine, or repeat responsibilities. It earns its place only if it clarifies what a section does and improves the shopper's next decision.

## Recognizable design language

- **Header/navigation:** calm utility rail, editorial brand plane, and a mobile navigation model designed around thumb-reachable progressive drill-in—not a desktop mega menu squeezed into a drawer.
- **Typography:** expressive display moments paired with highly legible commerce text; constrained scale roles rather than arbitrary sizes.
- **Spacing:** three semantic densities (Focused, Balanced, Airy) mapped to a token system.
- **Product cards:** a stable “fact rail” for price/variants/status plus a reserved narrative region; collection promo tiles share proportions without impersonating products.
- **Product media:** composed “chapters,” with predictable mobile paging and no competing sticky layers.
- **PDP:** purchase panel remains factual and efficient while story chapters progressively reveal benefits, usage, material/ingredients, proof and FAQs.
- **Collections:** product rhythm can be interrupted by clearly non-product editorial modules without corrupting pagination or filtering semantics.
- **Campaigns:** five initial commercial jobs (Product Launch, Paid Landing Page, Collection Launch, Editorial Story, Product Education) begin as native Shopify compositions using shared sections with purposeful presets.
- **Cart:** a quiet task surface; recommendations are subordinate and app-extensible.
- **Motion:** transitions clarify state/chapter changes, remain subtle, and disappear under reduced-motion preference.

## Architectural originality

Originality is the coupling of architecture, merchant workflow, semantic controls, storefront decision flow, preset system, mobile behavior, and structured-content strategy: semantic tokens; a shallow block taxonomy; optional data recipes; context-specific compositions; job-based editor presets; mobile content priorities; and tested missing-data fallbacks. Beauty & Wellness supplies the first concrete vocabulary and defaults, while contracts remain about durable commerce intent rather than ingredients-only concepts. Competitors can reproduce a section; the hypothesis is that reproducing the whole workflow requires more than changing styling or JSON order.

Shopify's verified current requirement is stronger than code provenance or a recognizable art direction: the submitted product must be fundamentally different from existing Theme Store themes through meaningful design and functional innovation at the architectural/overall-experience level. Cosmetic changes, extra settings, extra sections, and superficial styling are insufficient.

## How the grammar changes the product

- **Section responsibilities:** every narrative section declares one primary shopper question and CTA relationship; “do everything” sections are rejected.
- **Default composition:** defaults establish decision order and deliberate omissions, rather than maximizing section count.
- **PDP:** Reveal orients; Explain covers benefits/use; Prove carries factual evidence; Compare helps selection only when honest data exists; Act keeps purchase state clear. Purchase correctness is never subordinated to storytelling.
- **Collections:** Reveal establishes the edit; editorial interruptions Explain or Prove without masquerading as products; cards and filters retain an obvious Act path.
- **Campaign pages:** job presets select an appropriate subset and rhythm. Paid Landing Page should be shorter and more action-weighted than Editorial Story.
- **Mobile:** the highest-value explanation and purchase action move earlier; repeated media and sticky layers are reduced rather than merely stacked.
- **Merchandising:** promotions connect a claim or story to a legitimate collection/product destination; scarcity is never fabricated.
- **Structured content:** optional dynamic sources can reuse benefits, usage, specifications, proof, and FAQs, but normal Shopify data always renders a polished baseline.

## Anti-copy controls

1. Designers must cite the user problem, not a competitor screenshot, in every component brief.
2. No third-party Liquid/CSS/JS is copied without license review and attribution.
3. Visual review compares silhouettes, hierarchy, interaction and mobile states—not just source code.
4. Maintain decision records and original Figma history.
5. A pre-submission originality audit compares all presets against leading listings.

## What is intentionally conventional

Checkout-bound actions, labels, focus behavior, variant selection, filters and cart semantics should be familiar. Originality must not make commerce harder. Native Shopify APIs and accessibility patterns are foundations, not brand differentiators.

## Proof required before implementation

Prototype the home-to-PDP-to-collection-to-campaign grammar in grayscale. Compare intent-led starts against a blank/default baseline; measure decision count, completion, time, reversals, and comprehension with five target merchants. Stress the same contracts with lightweight apparel and food/beverage content. If the experience is only distinguishable by imagery/font, terminology, or JSON ordering—or cross-vertical use requires generic controls—return to product strategy and narrow if necessary.

## Weaknesses requiring validation

The current originality case is plausible, not proven. Narrative sequencing is easy to imitate; native presets are available to every theme; semantic settings can become mere relabeling; and Beauty & Wellness art direction may carry perceived quality. **This is a blocking M1 decision:** intent-led templates, Launch Narrative terminology, semantic settings, presets, or different JSON ordering alone are explicitly insufficient. Evidence must show a fundamentally different architecture and overall experience, meaningful functional/design innovation, faster merchant tasks, materially different mobile decisions, coherent cross-surface reuse, and code provenance. Failure means redesign or no-build—not M2. No document here claims Theme Store approval.
