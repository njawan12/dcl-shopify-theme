## Baseline versus differentiation

Necessary commerce basics must be reliable, accessible and fast, but they are not positioning claims. Swatches, quick buy, navigation, search, filters, recommendations and media support should be considered against real merchant needs and Shopify-native capabilities during later planning. The differentiating investment remains composition quality, comprehensible controls, mobile decision flow and coherent structured storytelling.

## Scope rule

A proposed capability enters the roadmap only when it has a named merchant job, evidence of frequency or value, a comprehensible control model, an acceptable mobile outcome and no better platform-native owner. “Competitor has it” is insufficient.
