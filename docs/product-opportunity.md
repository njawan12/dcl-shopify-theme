# Product opportunity

## Current decision

**PROCEED, NARROWED — to M1 product/design validation, not production implementation.**

The old "merchant launch workflow" hypothesis is retired. M0 evidence shows routine product creation/template assignment is generally already manageable in native Shopify. The recurring opportunity observed through DCL work is different: growing DTC brands repeatedly pay designers/developers when their current theme cannot express the storefront structure, purchase presentation, content composition or merchant controls they want.

## Target

Primary launch target: **design-conscious health, beauty and wellness DTC brands**, especially focused catalogs where PDP quality, product education, repeated visual iteration and purchase presentation matter.

Deliberate non-targets for the initial product:
- broad marketplace/department-store catalogs;
- B2B/wholesale-first storefronts;
- merchants needing app-like business logic inside the theme;
- merchants seeking a universal page builder;
- merchants whose primary need is the cheapest possible theme;
- verticals that require a schema/architecture fork before the Beauty/Wellness system is proven.

Adjacent verticals are future evidence questions, not launch requirements.

## Product thesis

Build a visually exceptional Beauty/Wellness-first Shopify theme informed by recurring agency customization work.

The product should give merchants/designers unusually useful control over high-value surfaces that repeatedly become custom-development tickets, while keeping the editor bounded, the storefront fast/accessibile, and the code predictable when bespoke work is still required.

Working internal promise:

> Agency-grade visual quality and useful control over the storefront changes growing DTC brands repeatedly ask developers to build.

This is not approved Theme Store listing copy.

## Value hierarchy

1. **Visual desirability** — the demo/storefront must sell the product before architecture claims matter.
2. **Useful native capability** — curated target-relevant commerce/storytelling features, not maximum feature count.
3. **Merchant/designer control** — materially extend what can be changed without source edits.
4. **Clean developer extensibility** — bespoke work remains legitimate and should be straightforward.
5. **Testability** — sensible high-value variables live in native customization where coherent; no custom testing/admin layer.

## What is table stakes

Do not claim differentiation from feature presence alone: badges, sticky ATC, cart drawer, metafields/dynamic sources, ingredients/usage/FAQ, galleries/video/before-after, swatches/options, filtering/sorting/quick buy, generic mobile-first language, large section counts or generic no-code/customizable claims.

## Surviving M1 hypotheses

### H1 — High-value structural flexibility
A bounded set of materially different compositions on surfaces brands repeatedly redesign can provide useful range without settings explosion.

### H2 — Agency-informed control selection
Recurring customization demand can be translated into a compact control system rather than exposing every implementation knob.

### H3 — Visual system + clean implementation
Beauty/Wellness visual identity can be strong enough to sell while the implementation remains clean enough to extend.

These remain hypotheses until M1.

## Why this product may exist

- DCL operator evidence reports recurring 2–3 day customization work around buy boxes, sections, structure/CSS, carts, product controls and related surfaces.
- Once appropriate controls are created, merchants generally operate them themselves.
- Strong incumbents already prove willingness to pay for premium visual quality/features, but their breadth does not eliminate recurring agency customization demand.
- Shopify's current uniqueness rules explicitly value deliberate industry targeting, inventive art direction and architectural differentiation.
- DCL's design/CRO/development experience is directly relevant to selecting useful defaults and controls.

## Reasons not to build

- Incumbents are mature, feature-rich and frequently praised for ease/support.
- Public evidence does not prove their editor-depth limits; some perceived gaps may disappear under hands-on use.
- A theme that is merely attractive plus configurable will not be unique enough.
- Demo art direction/content is a serious production investment.
- Support/maintenance is an ongoing product operation, not passive revenue.
- Too much flexibility can reproduce agency complexity inside the editor.
- Theme Store approval remains Shopify's decision.

## M1 entry conditions

M1 may begin only when:
- the revised M0 closure matrix is accepted;
- current official requirements are revalidated enough for prototype architecture;
- founder accepts Beauty/Wellness-first target and deliberate non-targets;
- founder accepts Theme Store-exclusive distribution;
- DCL accepts ongoing support/bug-fix/docs/demo obligations and identifies ownership before release;
- historical customization inventory and incumbent challenge are captured honestly;
- no unresolved evidence limitation is falsely converted into a competitive claim.

Independent merchant evidence remains valuable and should be collected opportunistically. It is not replaced by fabricated interview counts.

## M1 success conditions

M1 must prove the six high-risk systems can produce at least three materially different Beauty/Wellness directions using one coherent architecture; preserve premium zero-setup states; stay within control budgets; remain merchant-operable; and pass a developer-extension exercise.

Failure means narrow or stop before M2. Do not rescue the thesis with more sections/settings.

## Commercial posture

One excellent preset is the default launch assumption until evidence justifies another. Final price is deferred until product quality/support economics are clearer.

The theme must stand alone. DCL customization can be optional upside but cannot be required for normal supported use.

## Current recommendation

**Proceed to M1 once the finite founder operating commitments are explicitly accepted.**

Do not start production Liquid/CSS/JS/JSON or import Skeleton merely because M0 supports continued investment.
