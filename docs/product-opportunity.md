## Recommendation

Build a **launch-oriented premium theme for beauty and personal-care DTC teams**, expandable to adjacent wellness and lifestyle presets. Position it as “from product truth to coordinated launch”: a merchant can create structured, optional product stories and reuse a consistent visual grammar across PDPs, collections, and campaign pages.

This is not a claim that native data is automatically synchronized everywhere; Liquid sections must expose deliberate dynamic sources and fallbacks. The promise is a designed workflow, not magic.

## Why this product should exist

- Table-stakes commerce and premium storytelling are often presented as separate modes; launch teams need both.
- Product education (benefits, usage, ingredients/materials, proof and FAQs) maps well to reusable native data while still permitting zero-setup product descriptions.
- Frequent launches create a repeated, measurable job where presets can reduce agency hours.
- A narrow initial art direction can be memorable while adjacent presets later reuse architecture.
- Workflow, accessibility and mobile quality are harder to commoditize than isolated sections.

## Reasons we should NOT build this product

- Current competitors may already execute the complete workflow; live teardown could erase the gap.
- DCL has no validated merchant interviews, sales data, support capacity model, or demo-content budget yet.
- Theme Store approval is uncertain, and exclusivity plus maintenance obligations constrain distribution.
- Structured content can feel like setup work; adoption may be lower than assumed.
- Beauty claims, subscriptions, reviews, bundles and loyalty are app territory; merchants may expect integrations the theme cannot own.
- A single polished preset is a major art-direction and content-production investment, not merely engineering.
- If DCL cannot fund ongoing browser/platform QA and support, it should not enter this business.

## Go/no-go conditions

Proceed to M1 only if: at least 8 target-merchant interviews show repeated launch/PDP maintenance pain; five moderated users can understand the proposed content model; current competitor teardown identifies at least three workflow gaps; DCL commits a named product owner, support owner, design lead and QA capacity; and official eligibility/requirements are revalidated.

## Business recommendation

**CHANGE DIRECTION, then BUILD conditionally.** Do not build a broad “premium high-volume DTC theme.” Validate and build the narrower launch operating system. Failure of any go/no-go condition means **DO NOT BUILD yet**.
